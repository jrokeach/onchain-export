Zerion Wallet Exporter

Scan wallets through the Zerion API and export current holdings.

Wallets

One wallet per line. Optional name before or after the address, separated by a comma or tab. Lines starting with # are ignored.

Options

Cost basis will likely be wrong. Zerion only sees each wallet's on-chain history and prices every token at the market rate on the day it arrived in that wallet. The figure is only correct if you landed funds on-chain in USD terms (e.g. as USDC) and swapped into each token from there, inside the scanned wallet. Tokens transferred in from your other wallets or an exchange, bridged, wrapped, or moved in and out of DeFi protocols get a new basis at the arrival price, and borrowed tokens have none.

Post-scan adjustments

Applied to the last scan instantly, without new Zerion requests. Saved in this browser.

Miscellaneous Tokens

Report as individual wallet

Tokens Range can't handle in a normal account. Each is reported as its own account named <account>:<ticker>, always with price and value (those columns stay in the output, filled for these rows, even if hidden above). Never folded into Miscellaneous Tokens.

Remap tickers

Report a token under another ticker (e.g. ASV-USD as USDC). Rows with the same new ticker are merged; a token Zerion has no price for takes the new ticker's price from this scan.
